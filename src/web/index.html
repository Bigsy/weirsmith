weirsmith

★ Star on GitHub

Harper's dictionary is bigger than you think. Bring your own dictionary files and this will tell you which words Harper is actually missing, help you tag them, and build a Weirpack.

Your files are read by your own browser and never leave this machine. There is no server to send them to.

1 Your dictionaries

Drop dictionary files here, or click to choose.

Plain word lists, hunspell .dic, and .gz of either. Include the .aff next to a .dic and its affix flags get translated into Harper's instead of dropped.

2 What Harper is missing

Every candidate is linted against a real Harper in all five dialects. This is the step that decides whether converting anything is worth it.

3 Tag the survivors

Flags are guessed from each word's shape. Graded against WordNet on 78,019 words the guess agrees 80% of the time, so expect to correct roughly one in five — that is what these controls are for. Fix a whole group at once, then override individual words.

4 Build the pack

Harper refuses to import a pack unless all four of these are present.
